# Project Pulse — Final Build 1.0

This branch is the production track for Project Pulse.

## Locked core
- Auto-run movement and jump feel from Alpha 0.6
- Two-reality Pulse mechanic
- Gradual difficulty and tempo escalation
- Adaptive cinematic/electronic soundtrack
- Perfect Pulse, Clutch Pulse, Near Miss and Overdrive
- Campaign ranks and Endless mode

## 1.0 target
- 16-stage campaign across four acts
- Distinct act mechanics and musical identity
- Persistent progression, ranks and best scores
- Clean first-run onboarding and settings
- Accessibility and reduced-effects support
- YouTube Playables SDK adapter with cloud saves, audio, pause/resume, readiness and score hooks
- Mobile and desktop responsive playfield
- Certification-oriented performance and bundle checks

## Campaign structure
- Act I — Ignition: Stages 1–4, teach the language of Jump + Pulse
- Act II — Pressure: Stages 5–8, bosses, moving threats, shields and blackout
- Act III — Fracture: Stages 9–12, denser rhythm patterns and new reality interactions
- Act IV — Collapse: Stages 13–16, mastery tests and final Hunter Prime encounter

Alpha 0.6 on `main` remains the known-good gameplay reference while final development proceeds on `final-1.0`.
